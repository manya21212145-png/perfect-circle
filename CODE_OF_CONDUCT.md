# Code of Conduct

Perfect Circle is a student and classroom project. Everyone who takes part (writing code, testing, filing issues
or reviewing) is expected to help keep it friendly and welcoming.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to coding.
- Give feedback about the work, not the person, and explain the reason behind a suggestion.
- Accept that decisions follow the project documents and the product owner's sign-off.
- Respect other people's time: describe problems clearly and follow the issue templates.

## Not acceptable

- Insults, harassment, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sharing someone else's private information, such as a PIN or personal details, without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting testing, duels or discussions.

## Reporting

If something makes you uncomfortable, contact the repository owner privately. Reports are handled confidentially.
The owner may remove comments, commits or issues that break this code, and may remove a contributor from the project.
